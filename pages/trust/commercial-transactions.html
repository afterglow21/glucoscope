The seller is a Japanese consumption-tax-exempt sole proprietor and is not a qualified invoice issuer. Forwarding and real receipt are confirmed.

Duplicate payments or a paid pass that cannot be started will first be corrected; if correction is not possible, the payment will be refunded in full. A major GlucoScope-side outage that made the main Plus features mostly unusable and could not be resolved may also receive a full refund after review. No partial refunds are offered, and a refunded pass ends.
