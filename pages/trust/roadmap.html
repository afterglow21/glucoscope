現在の技術課題

Friends & Family Preview、AI利用カウンター、共有KVキャッシュ、CORS制限、Trust Pack、Web Analytics、初回告知まで進みました。一般利用者向けの最初の実機確認では、途中画面をまとめたガイドだけでは一人で進めにくく、SKIPする画面とアプリの見分け方を一画面ずつ案内する必要があることが分かりました。現在は、Glurooの全画面チェックポイントと、FreeStyle LibreLink・LibreLinkUp・Glurooを見分ける案内へ改訂しています。

既存Nightscoutのブラウザ直接接続は確認済みです。Gluroo Global Connectは、接続先と取得内容を限定したCloudflare Worker、Guardian MonitorからGlurooへの実機送信、Turnstile、約1時間の接続チケット、回数制限まで完成しました。2026年8月6日、Glurooから、EULA等と矛盾しない範囲で今回の使い方は動作し問題ないとの文書回答を受け取り、医療判断、CGMデータ再共有、料金、非提携、問い合わせ責任の境界を公開文面へ反映しました。同日、別の明示的な確認を挟んで限定中継を一時有効化し、Guardian MonitorからGluroo、限定中継、GlucoScopeまでの全経路で、iPhone Safariの現在血糖・グラフ表示と再読み込み後の再表示を確認しました。確認後はすぐ`RELAY_ENABLED=false`へ戻し、停止版、正確なCORS、Secret名、Durable Objectを再確認しました。現在は停止中です。次は期間別・期限切れ・削除・上限の追加確認と、Friends & Familyで継続有効化するかの別の判断です。確認が終わっていないLibre・Dexcomなどは確認済みとは案内しません。

User Foundation

The current phase is revising onboarding from real-device feedback: each optional screen needs an explicit step, app switches need exact app identification, and a participant should be able to proceed without live coaching.

Existing Nightscout browser access is verified. The Gluroo-only relay has Worker reachability, a Guardian Monitor input route, server-side Turnstile, short-lived tickets, request limits, a stopped Worker shell, a Durable Object, the required Secret bindings, and one public target. On 2026-08-06, Gluroo replied in writing that the proposed use should work and was acceptable only while it remains consistent with their EULA, terms, and other documents. The public boundary records the medical-decision restriction, no legal assurance about CGM re-sharing, no marketing as a free subscription-Nightscout alternative, possible future GGC subscription, non-affiliation, and GlucoScope-owned support responsibility. A separately approved temporary enablement then completed the first Guardian path from Guardian Monitor through Gluroo, the relay, and GlucoScope in iPhone Safari; current glucose, graph display, and display after reload passed. The relay was returned immediately to `RELAY_ENABLED=false`, and the stopped Version, exact CORS, Secret names, and Durable Object binding were rechecked. Extended period, expiry, deletion, and limit checks and a separate continuing-enablement decision remain before a low-volume Friends & Family rollout. Libre, Dexcom, and other routes will not be described as verified until their own checks pass.

Community & Sustainability

One-time optional development support is now available without changing features or access. GlucoScope will continue to grow with the community and explore ethical ways to keep the project running.
